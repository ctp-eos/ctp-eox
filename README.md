## 🌌 CTP-EOX Linux — The CTP Tesseract Reimagined

**CTP-EOX** is a rebranded fork and interpretation of [Mabox Linux](https://maboxlinux.org) — a minimalist, Arch-based distro focused on extremely low CPU overhead and fast responsiveness.

We have adapted Mabox to fit seamlessly within the **CTP/CTP-EOS total ecosystem**, aligning it with the aesthetic, values, and scientific principles of **CTPSci Sciences**.

---

## Why CTP-EOX?

CTP-EOX represents more than just a lightweight Linux flavor. It embodies the philosophy of **efficiency, clarity, and conscious design** that underpins the broader CTP Energy Science/CTPSci Sciences vision.

- **Minimal resource consumption:** Ideal for modern and legacy hardware alike, maximizing speed while preserving power
- **CTPSci Aesthetic:** Every visual element — from boot splash to desktop wallpaper — is curated to reflect the vibrant CTP “Tesseract” motif and brand identity
- **Ecosystem integration:** As part of the CTP-EOX branch of CTP-EOS Linux, it shares compatibility and extensibility, enabling seamless adoption and innovation
- **Tailored for creators and experimenters:** With `jgmenu` as the default launcher and options for audio production tools like Renoise themes, it supports deep customization without bloat

This fork allows us to leverage Mabox’s rock-solid base while crafting a focused, branded experience designed to accelerate productivity and conscious workflows in the CTP community.
